Featured Work

Permitting Activity in Orleans Parish

Data analysis & storytelling · New Orleans construction permits

An independent analysis of construction permitting trends in Orleans Parish, Louisiana, built from the City of New Orleans open data portal (data.nola.gov). The project cleans and merges the city's raw permit and building datasets, then asks a straightforward question with a rigorous answer: how has construction activity actually changed before and after 2020, and where?

  • Cleaned and validated raw permit data, correcting for text-formatted currency values, per-unit vs. per-project value duplication, and inconsistent neighborhood naming.
  • Ran both OLS and Theil-Sen trend tests on monthly permitting activity, re-testing with edge months dropped to confirm results held up.
  • Compared pre-pandemic (2015–2019) and settled recent (2022+) periods by neighborhood, identifying where construction activity grew, held steady, or declined.
  • Examined construction value concentration by neighborhood and tested whether decline tracked distance from the Central Business District.
